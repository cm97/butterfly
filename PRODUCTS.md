Tips for making products worth buying:

1. **Name the outcome, not the file.** "Weekly Planner" sells worse than "Stop Missing Deadlines".
2. **One sentence of proof.** Even "Built from 3 years of my own messy notes" beats nothing.
3. **Price in a range people don't flinch at.** $9–$49 is the sweet spot for solo digital products.
4. **The crossed-out price is doing heavy lifting** — it makes the real price feel like a steal.
5. **FAQ answers are where sales are won or lost.** Write them like you're talking to a skeptical friend.
